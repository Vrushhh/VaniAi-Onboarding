We don't just automate calls. We build your iconic AI voice workforce.

Each agent runs a job you'd otherwise staff a calling team for - from the first order confirmation to the last payment nudge.

Orders

Order confirmation

Verify COD and prepaid orders within minutes of checkout. Cut RTO before it ships.

"Hi there! Calling to confirm your order #4821. Will tomorrow afternoon be convenient for delivery?"
Sales

Lead qualification

Call every new lead while it's warm. Ask your qualifying questions, score, and route to your team.

"Hello! Thanks for reaching out. What is your monthly order volume so I can suggest the best plan for you?"
Retention

Win-back & renewals

Reach lapsed customers and expiring subscriptions with a personal call, not another ignored SMS.

"Hi! Your subscription is expiring this weekend. Would you like me to renew your plan today with a 20% discount?"
Collections

Payment follow-up

Polite, consistent EMI and invoice reminders that keep relationships intact - and money moving.

"Hello! A quick friendly reminder that your monthly invoice is due. Should I send an instant payment link to your phone?"
Customer Success

After-Sales Support

Post-delivery calls that customers actually answer. Structured feedback lands in your dashboard.

"Hi! Hope you received your order safely! How would you rate our delivery service on a scale of 1 to 10?"
Logistics

Delivery coordination

Confirm addresses, reschedule attempts, and reduce failed deliveries before the courier gives up.

"Hello! Your courier driver is at your location. Should I update a new delivery time for you?"
